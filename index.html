Welcome to the prototype website of Semitica Aberdonensia.

The aim of Semitica Aberdonensia is to document and present the history of the scholarly engagement with the Semitic languages and related cultures at academic institutions in Aberdeen, in the north-east of Scotland, from the foundation of its first university in 1495 to the present day.

The website also provides information on the academic events taking place in Aberdeen, whether in-person, online, or in hybrid format, which are related to the study of the Hebrew Bible / Old Testament and the various fields of Semitic Studies.
