Old Testament/Hebrew Bible Seminar, autumn term 2026/27

The Seminar will meet every other Wednesday (with the exception of Tuesday, 10 November), starting on 30 September. Staff, PGR and PGT students from all subjects, as well as advanced UG students, are cordially invited to attend any or all of the sessions. They take place in hybrid format, in the Humanity Manse seminar room and online.
