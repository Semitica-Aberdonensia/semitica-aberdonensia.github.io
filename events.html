Old Testament/Hebrew Bible Seminar

Autumn Term 2026/27

The Seminar will meet every other Wednesday (with the exception of Tuesday, 10 November), starting on 30 September 2026. Faculty, PhD and Postgraduate / Graduate Master's students from all subjects, as well as advanced Undergraduate students, are cordially invited to attend any or all of the sessions. They take place in hybrid format, in the Humanity Manse seminar room in Old Aberdeen and online. The link will be shared with registered participants.

Convener: Professor Joachim Schaper
